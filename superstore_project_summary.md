The superstore project analyzes sales performance and profitability across different business dimensions.  Using Python, pandas, seaborn, matplotlib, and Jupyter Notebook, the analysis explore sales patterns, profit distribution, category and sub-category performance, regional differences, time trends, and discount effects. It also includes cross-dimensional analysis to examine how profit varies across different factors. The dataset contains 9,994 rows and 12 columns, with no missing values. The outcomes show that sales are highly right-skewed, indicating that most orders have relatively low sales values. Profit is concentrated around zero and fluctuates significantly over time. Among all regions, the West region has the highest number of orders, highest total sales, and the highest total profit. The analysis also shows that profitability varies across categories and sub-categories. In addition, higher discount levels are associated with lower profit, and orders with discounts above 0.4 are likely to result in negative profit.
